## 4. Abstract, Main Text & Supplementary Notes Quantitative Claims

Every headline numerical claim in the Abstract, Results, Methods, and Supplementary Notes 1–6 is verified automatically by `python scripts/reproduce_results.py --quick` (`src.summary_stats.verify_manuscript_claims`) and unit tests in `tests/`:

- **Abstract & Results (Compiler)**: 7 binary primitives compile `2` to `48` strategies and `70` distinct games in every mechanism family (`70` distinct games and up to `135` strategies in the threshold grammar; `45` strategies at RN24). Verified in `tests/test_compiler_and_payoffs.py` and `paper_numbers.json`.
- **Results & SI Note 1 (Lattice collapse & digest identity)**: Inert primitive counts out of 64 profiles: `LTG=32`, `LAG=36`, `DAG=12`, `RET=12`, `OPT=OBS=ANTI=0`; payoff tensor max difference across identical digests = `0.0` over all `280` distinct games (`extra_checks.json`).
- **Results & SI Note 2 (Payoff & solver verification)**: Exhaustive two-type group check across `615,870` comparisons (`max_abs_err = 3.6e-15`), random parameter check across `9,600` groups (`max_abs_err = 2.8e-14`), and 60-digit `mpmath` stationary distribution agreement `< 2e-16` (`1.1e-16` max).
- **Abstract & Results (Peer-punishment map & disagreement allocation)**: Max cooperation `0.916` (node `1100010`); compulsory (`OPT=0`) max `< 2e-10` (`1.04e-10`); cooperation falls from `0.913` (`0.91`) at H4 to `0.321` (`0.32`) at RN24 (drop `0.591`), allocated under uniform mutation as `LTG = -0.248` (`42%`), `DAG = -0.160` (`27%`), `LAG = -0.113` (`19%`), `ANTI = -0.070` (`12%`).
- **Results & SI Note 4 (Neutral benchmark & mutation kernels)**: Neutral (`w=0`) total difference $= 1/3 - 1/2 = -1/6$, allocated as `LTG = +0.065`, `ANTI = +0.065`, `DAG = -0.148`, `LAG = -0.148`. Action-balanced kernel keeps ranking (`LTG -0.245`); single-trait reset reverses `LTG` (`+0.042`, `DAG -0.127`); redraw ranks `LAG` most negative (`-0.201`).
- **Results & SI Note 6 (Cross-society check)**: 16 pools, 15 countries, 1,120 punishment-condition participants, `2,062` of `3,360` period-1 pairs eligible for antisocial punishment; P1 $\tau_b = -0.48$ ($P_{\text{Holm}} = 0.015$), P4 $\tau_b = -0.58$ ($P_{\text{Holm}} = 0.0022$), negative cooperation gain in Athens, Muscat, and Riyadh.
